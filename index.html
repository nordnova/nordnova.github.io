A half-empty kindergarten, filled in one campaign.

How we did it
  1. Audit & research. A full marketing audit, plus research into the market and what parents want.
  2. Strategy. The marketing plan, and the school's unique selling points sharpened into clear messages.
  3. Online. A new website, a Google Business Profile, and Meta ads in colloquial Jordanian Arabic led by all-inclusive pricing.
  4. Offline. School decoration, signage, roll-ups, flyers and new campus photography.
  5. People. Teacher feedback and parent communication, so the school experience matched the promise.

Kindergarten went from 61 to 120 seats, and cost per enquiry fell to roughly a third.
